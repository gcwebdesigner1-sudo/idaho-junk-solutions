Before you book

    

      
What’s included in the load price?
Labor, disposal fees, travel within our service cities and cleanup. Specialty items and services are priced separately.

      
How do I know which load size I have?
Text us a photo and we’ll estimate it. We confirm the size and price on site before we start, so you can say no before anything is loaded.

      
Can you come today?
Often, yes. Same-day pickup depends on the schedule, so call or text early in the day.

      
What costs extra?
Refrigeration units ($30 to $50), tires ($10 to $25), and single items over 250 lbs ($40). Concrete, dirt, sand and rock are priced by the cubic yard.

      
Do you remove hot tubs and pianos?
Yes. Hot tub removal runs $350 to $550 and piano removal starts at $250. A hardwired hot tub needs an electrician to disconnect it first.

      
What can’t you take?
We don’t handle biohazard removal or disposal. If you’re not sure about an item, send us a photo and ask.
